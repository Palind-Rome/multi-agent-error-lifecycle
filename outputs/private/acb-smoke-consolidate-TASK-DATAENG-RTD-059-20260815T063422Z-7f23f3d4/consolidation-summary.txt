## ✅ **Current Progress & Key Decisions Made**

- **Pipeline Design Finalized:**  
  The customer interaction data pipeline is fully designed with end-to-end compliance enforcement.
- **Critical Constraints Enforced:**  
  - `[ANON-HASH-PII]`: All PII fields (e.g., `user_id`, `email`, `ip_address`) are tokenized using **salted SHA-256** at ingestion.

## 🔑 **Key Context, Constraints & User Preferences**

- **Non-Negotiables:**  
  - No raw PII may exist in any layer (raw, staging, warehouse).  
  - Purge must be **exact 93 days**, no rounding or buffer zones.  
  - Salt must be **rotated quarterly**, stored securely (AWS KMS/HashiCorp Vault), never hardcoded.
- **Governance by Design:**  
  All controls embedded in schema, code, and orchestration — not bolted on.
- **Monitoring & Audit:**  
  Datadog alerts for purge failure, anonymization error rate >0.1%, DLQ backlog. Audit logs retained for 7 years.
